Official minimum

The scholarship publishes this threshold, for example GKS (80% or 2.64/4), Türkiye Bursları (70% / 75% / 80%), ETH (top 10%). Below it, you can't apply.

Typical competitive GPA

No official minimum is published, so this shows the profile that usually wins (an estimate). You can still apply below it if the rest of your file is strong.

GPA isn't everything

Research, leadership, a strong essay and good references often outweigh a few tenths of a GPA point.
