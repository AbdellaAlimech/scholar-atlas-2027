Privacy policy

Scholar Atlas 2027 · last updated 26 November 2026

Summary

Scholar Atlas 2027 does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your grade, filters, language, saved scholarships, application statuses and letter-builder answers are stored only on your own device (in the browser's local storage), so the app remembers them next time. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Connections the app makes

  • It loads the app files and the scholarship list from GitHub Pages, where the app is hosted.
  • It loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address as part of that request, under Google's privacy policy.
  • When you tap a scholarship's official website or "Google Calendar", that site opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 16 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Contact

Questions or corrections: open an issue on GitHub.
